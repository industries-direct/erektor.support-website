# How-To Guides & Walkthroughs

Welcome to the comprehensive how-to guides for Erektor equipment. Select a guide below to learn about operations, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

## Available Guides

### [Controller Operations & Setup Guide](./controller-guide.html)

Learn how to set up, operate, and maintain your Erektor controller system.

**Topics covered:**
- Initial setup and installation
- Power-on procedures and daily operations
- Using preset positions and movement controls
- Advanced features (calibration, remote operation, logging)
- Troubleshooting common issues
- Error code reference
- Maintenance schedules

**Difficulty Level**: Beginner to Intermediate

---

### [Leg Maintenance & Replacement Guide](./leg-replacement.html)

Comprehensive guide to maintaining and replacing equipment legs.

**Topics covered:**
- Safety information and procedures
- Leg anatomy and components
- Routine daily, weekly, and monthly maintenance
- Diagnosing and troubleshooting leg issues
- Field replacement procedures
- When to replace legs
- Service life expectations
- Maintenance schedules

**Difficulty Level**: Beginner to Advanced
